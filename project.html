Interactive 3D Works Portfolio Exhibition Hall

3D exhibition hall scene, with images and project cards placed on the page. The mouse wheel controls forward and backward movement, dragging left or right rotates the viewing angle, and clicking on an exhibit brings up a detail popup window or video preview.
Technology: HTML, CSS, JS, Three.js (many beginner templates)
Feature List:
1. 3D exhibition room model with artwork cards displayed on the walls
2. Drag to rotate the view and scroll to roam
3. Click on artwork to pop up details (description, screenshots, links)
4. Dark-themed, atmospheric UI with background music toggle

Feature List:
1. Mouse position drives particle movement and gravitational effects
>2. Particle color and density can switch between preset themes (cool blue / cyber purple)

4. Toggle button: turn particle effect on / off
